What you hold at the end

A record that says what you did.

Finish a pathway and Health Matters Clinic issues a completion record naming the pathway, the coursework, the assessment you passed and the date. Where you served in the field, the hours are on it.

Being straight about what it is not: California does not have a state Community Health Worker certification, and the Department of Health Care Services does not approve certificates. Nobody can issue you one, including us. What an HMC completion record does is document real coursework and real hours to an employer or a program, which is what most of them ask for.

Questions

What learners ask before they enroll.

Cost and access
Is it really free?

Yes, and it stays free. There is no paid tier, no certificate fee and no upgrade. Every community-facing Health Matters Clinic service is free.

Free to receive is not free to run. Course production, staffing and materials are paid for by sponsors, grants and donors.

Do I need an account?

To take a course, yes, because your progress and completion record have to attach to someone. To save a spot in a cohort, no. That takes an email address and creates nothing.

Who can enroll?

Anyone. Health Careers Exploration is written for ages 14 and up. The youth pathway is for ages 12 to 26. The professional and facilitator tracks are open to anyone already working in or entering community health.

Courses and credentials
What do I get at the end?

A Health Matters Clinic completion record naming the pathway, the coursework, the assessment you passed and the date. Where you served in the field, the hours are on it.

Does this certify me as a Community Health Worker?

No, and be careful of anyone who says theirs does. California has no state Community Health Worker certification, and the Department of Health Care Services does not approve certificates. What an HMC completion record does is document real coursework and real hours, which is what employers and programs generally ask to see.

How long does a pathway take?

Health Careers Exploration is eight courses, 24 lessons and about seven hours of content. Self-paced means you set the pace; a guided cohort spreads it across several weeks with a group.

Why do some courses say in curriculum review?

Because they are not finished, and we would rather say so than list them as available. When a course is open it says open, and every lesson unlocks the moment you enroll.

Bringing this to your group
Can you train our staff or members?

Yes. The Unstoppable curriculum has been delivered to community groups and presented to the Los Angeles County Department of Mental Health, and the facilitator track exists so your own people can deliver it.

Start at healthmatters.clinic/contact-us.

Do you offer continuing education credit?

Continuing education sessions are scheduled through the portal and the boards they are approved for are named on each session. Where a session carries no approval, it says so.
